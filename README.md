## Suggested Reading Order

The chapters follow a standard AI curriculum progression:

**Foundations:** Chapters 1–2 (what AI is, agents, rationality)

**Classical Search:** Chapters 3–6 (problem formulation, uninformed search, informed search, local search/optimization)

**Beyond Classical Search:** Chapters 7–8 (uncertainty, online search, adversarial search/game playing)

**Logical Reasoning:** Chapters 9–12 (knowledge-based agents, propositional logic, first-order logic, inference, Prolog)

**Probabilistic Reasoning:** Chapters 13–14 (probability, Bayes' rule, Bayesian networks, inference, learning)

**Machine Learning:** Chapter 15 (supervised learning, decision trees, neural networks, evaluation, generalization)

---

Each chapter file is self-contained and can be read independently, though later chapters build on concepts introduced earlier (agents in Chapter 2 underpin search in Chapters 3–8; logic in Chapters 9–12 underpins knowledge representation; probability in Chapters 13–14 underpins the learning discussion in Chapter 15).
