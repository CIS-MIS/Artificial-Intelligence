◼belief state = {1},...,{N}, {1,2},...,{1,N}, {1,2,3}, ...,..., {1,2,....,N}
◼Why use belief states?
❑Can handle sensorless problem
◼Agent can fully observe its own belief state !
◼Can find a solution as a sequence of actions
❑Can also handle non -deterministic problem
◼No contingency plan needed
