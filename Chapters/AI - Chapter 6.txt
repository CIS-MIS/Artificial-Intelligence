3/5. Simulated Annealing
35◼Rolling a ball on a bumpy surface, trying to find  
the lowest point
❑Momentum can move it out of local minima
❑Shake the surface to move it out of local minima
◼Instead of taking “best” move, take random move
❑If new state is better, accept move
❑If new state is worse, accept with some probability <1
❑Probability of accepting worse point is determined by  
“temperature”
➔ escape local maxima by allowing some "bad" moves but 
gradually decrease  their frequency
36Annealing : Heat a metal to a high temperature and then gradually cool it, 
allowing the material to reach a low -energy crystalline state so it is hardened.
• Start by shaking hard (i.e., at 
   high temperature).
• Gradually reduce the intensity
   of shaking (i.e., lower the 
   temperature).

4. Otherwise, keep the 𝑘 best successors

5/5. Genetic algorithm
◼Idea: mimic evolution in biology
◼Similar to stochastic beam search
❑Keep k best successors with some randomness
❑Survival of the fittest: decide which descendants to  
keep by an objective (=fitness) function
❑Use two states, instead of one, to generate new states

Genetic  algorithm
41◼A successor state is generated by combining two parent 
states
◼Start with k randomly generated states ( population )
◼A state is represented as a string over a finite alphabet 
(often a string of 0s and 1s)
◼Evaluation function ( fitness function ). Higher values for 
better states.
◼Produce the next generation of states by selection, 
crossover, and mutation
How genetic algorit hmswork
42◼Encode a state by a string:
◼8-queens: (8 positions per queen)  * (3bit per position) = 24bits
◼One string = one state = an individual
◼Population: k individuals
◼New generation from previous generation
❑Crossover: exchange parts of strings from two parents
❑Mutation: change bits randomly forexploration
❑Selection: remove unfit individuals
How genetic algorit hmswork

◼Scheduling: How much time saved?
❑Fitness determines the probability that the genes of an  
individual will be carried on into the next generation
